1. What is the difference between RTM and RPM?

Answer:
Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM) focuses on therapy adherence and patient-reported health data between visits. It is designed to help providers monitor whether patients are following prescribed therapeutic programs over time. Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), by contrast, is centered on physiological data collected from connected devices, such as blood pressure or glucose readings. RTM is especially valuable for musculoskeletal care, rehabilitation, post-surgical recovery, and other therapy-driven treatment plans.

4. Why is patient engagement so important in RTM?

Answer:
Patient engagement is what makes RTM effective. If patients stop participating, monitoring loses value. That is why NXT Medical incorporates structured, game-based therapeutic activities and ongoing support to help patients stay involved over time. Consistent engagement improves adherence, reduces drop-off, and gives providers better visibility into real-world patient activity between visits.
