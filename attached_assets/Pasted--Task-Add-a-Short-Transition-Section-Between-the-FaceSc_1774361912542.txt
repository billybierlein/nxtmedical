# Task: Add a Short Transition Section Between the FaceScan Section and the Game-Based Engagement Section

## Objective
Add a short transitional content section between:

1. **How We Turn Patient Progress Into Measurable, Billable Data**  
and  
2. **Keeping Patients Engaged Is What Drives Results**

This transition section should help connect the story of the page:

- FaceScan = objective measurement
- engagement activities = ongoing participation
- together = a stronger RTM program

The goal is to create a smoother narrative flow so the reader understands that:
**measurement alone is not enough — sustained patient engagement is what makes monitoring effective over time.**

---

## Strategic Purpose
This section should act as a bridge, not a full standalone feature section.

It should:
- reinforce the relationship between monitoring and engagement
- prepare the reader for the next section
- improve page flow and readability
- make the overall RTM model feel more complete and intentional

This section should be concise and elegant.

---

## Section Placement
Insert this section directly:

- **after the FaceScan / measurable data section**
- **before the game-based engagement section**

Page flow should become:

1. What NXT Medical Provides in RTM
2. How We Turn Patient Progress Into Measurable, Billable Data
3. **NEW: Transition / bridge section**
4. Keeping Patients Engaged Is What Drives Results
5. CTA / demo section

---

## Important Design Direction
This should **not** look like another major feature section.

Do not use:
- a full card grid
- a large dense layout
- a repeated boxed format from surrounding sections

Instead, use a **simple transitional layout** such as one of these:
- a centered statement block with supporting text
- a split two-column text layout
- a short horizontal “measurement + engagement = outcome” visual row
- a minimal 3-step process strip

Preferred direction:
A clean, centered content block with a short visual support row beneath it.

This should feel like a brief pause in the page that helps the user connect the dots.

---

## Recommended Content Structure

### Option A (Preferred)
- small eyebrow label
- concise headline
- 1 short paragraph
- 3 mini supporting points in a single row

This is likely the best fit because it adds clarity without overwhelming the page.

### Why This Approach Works

Suggested styling:

Possible styling directions:

## Layout Requirements

### Main content
- centered within standard page container

### Support row
Desktop:
