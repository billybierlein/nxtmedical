# Task: Add New RTM Technology Differentiation Section to Website

## Objective
Add a new website section directly **below the existing “What NXT Medical Provides in RTM” section**.

This new section should explain:

1. **How the technology works**
2. **Why it benefits the provider/patient**
3. **Why this is unique when working with NXT Medical**

The goal is to make the technology feel:
- credible
- easy to understand
- clinically valuable
- operationally simple for the provider

---

## Important Design Direction
Do **not** repeat the exact same 6-card grid layout used in the section above.

Reason:
- The section above already communicates feature/service categories
- This new section should feel more like a **differentiation / explanation block**
- We want visual variety and better content hierarchy

Instead, use a **new section format** with:
- a centered heading + subheading
- a short intro paragraph
- **3 horizontally aligned content cards on desktop**
- stacked vertically on mobile
- each card representing:
  1. How It Works
  2. Why It’s More Effective
  3. Why NXT Is Different

---

## Section Placement
Insert this section immediately **after** the existing RTM services/features section shown on the page.

It should visually feel like the next logical story section:
- first section = what we provide
- new section = why this approach is better

Add generous top and bottom spacing so it does not feel cramped.

Suggested spacing:
- `py-20` desktop
- `py-14` mobile

---

## Visual Style
Match the overall brand and style of the current page:
- clean medical / professional look
- light background or very subtle contrast background
- rounded cards
- soft border or soft shadow
- readable typography
- calm, trustworthy tone

Suggested styling direction:
- section background: white or very light gray
- cards: white with light border
- rounded corners: `rounded-2xl`
- padding: generous (`p-6` to `p-8`)
- subtle shadow only if already consistent with rest of page
- icons optional but helpful if already used elsewhere on page

---

## Layout Requirements

### Section wrapper
- centered container
- max width similar to existing content sections
- maintain consistent left/right padding with the rest of the page

### Top content
Include:
- eyebrow or small label optional
- large section headline
- 1–2 sentence supporting copy
- short intro paragraph before the cards

### Card layout
Desktop:
- 3 columns
Tablet:
- 2 columns if needed, with final card wrapping cleanly
Mobile:
- 1 column

Each card should have:
- short title
- concise paragraph or short bullet-style content
- consistent spacing
- equal visual weight

---

## Copy to Use

### Section Heading
## How We Turn Patient Progress Into Measurable, Billable Data

### Supporting Intro
Most monitoring programs rely on subjective feedback.

NXT Medical uses advanced AI-based facial scanning technology to objectively track patient progress — giving you real clinical insight while supporting compliant RTM billing.

---

### Card 1 Title
### How It Works

### Card 1 Copy
Patients complete a quick scan using their smartphone or tablet — no equipment, no learning curve.

Our system analyzes subtle facial movements and neuromuscular patterns to generate measurable data on patient function and response to therapy.

Each scan creates a baseline, allowing us to track changes over time and identify meaningful progress or setbacks.

---

### Card 2 Title
### Why This Is More Effective

### Card 2 Copy
Traditional care relies heavily on patient-reported symptoms, in-office assessments, and limited visibility between visits.

With NXT:
- Progress is objectively measured, not estimated
- Monitoring happens continuously, not just in-office
- Small issues can be identified before they become setbacks
- Each interaction contributes to documented clinical value

---

### Card 3 Title
### Why This Is Unique to NXT Medical

### Card 3 Copy
Most RTM programs stop at data collection.

NXT delivers a complete, done-for-you system:
- We manage patient engagement and monitoring
- We structure the data into actionable progress reports
- We support documentation aligned with RTM billing requirements
- Your team stays focused on patient care and clinical decisions

No new workflows. No additional staff. No extra burden.

---

### Optional Closing Statement
Add a centered line beneath the cards if it fits the design well:

**You get the benefits of advanced remote monitoring — without changing how your practice operates.**

Use slightly emphasized styling, but keep it elegant and not oversized.

---

## UX / Content Rules
- Keep line lengths readable
- Do not make paragraphs too dense
- Break content into short paragraphs or compact bullet groups where helpful
- Preserve a premium, trustworthy healthcare tone
- Avoid hypey or overly “techy” language
- Make the section easy for chiropractors and other providers to understand quickly

---

## Responsive Requirements
Ensure this section looks polished on:
- desktop
- tablet
- mobile

Specifically:
- no overly wide text blocks
- clean spacing between heading, intro, cards, and closing line
- cards should stack naturally on smaller screens
- no awkward empty gaps

---

## Accessibility / Quality
- Maintain semantic heading structure
- Ensure contrast is strong enough for readability
- If icons are used, they should support content and not distract
- Avoid clutter
- Keep padding and typography consistent with the rest of the site

---

## Definition of Done
This task is complete when:
1. A new section is added directly below the current RTM services section
2. It does **not** reuse the same 6-card grid style
3. It clearly communicates:
   - how the technology works
   - why it is beneficial
   - why NXT is different
4. It is fully responsive and visually aligned with the rest of the site
5. The content is easy to scan and professionally presented
6. The section strengthens trust and differentiation on the RTM page
