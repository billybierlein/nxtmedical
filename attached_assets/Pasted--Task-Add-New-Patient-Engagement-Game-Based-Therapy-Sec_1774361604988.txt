# Task: Add New Patient Engagement / Game-Based Therapy Section to RTM Website Page

## Objective
Add a new section to the RTM website page that explains why **structured game-based therapeutic activities** are an important part of the NXT Medical RTM program.

This section should communicate:

1. Why patient engagement matters in RTM
2. How game-based activities help improve consistency and outcomes
3. Why this approach is different and more effective with NXT Medical

The goal is to position this part of the program as:
- clinically meaningful
- easy to understand
- research-supported
- differentiated from generic monitoring programs

---

## Section Placement
Insert this section **after the FaceScan / technology differentiation section** and **before the main CTA/demo section**.

Recommended narrative flow on page:

1. What NXT Medical Provides in RTM
2. How We Turn Patient Progress Into Measurable, Billable Data
3. **NEW: Patient Engagement / Game-Based Therapy section**
4. CTA / Demo / Next Step

This new section should feel like the next logical step in the story:
- section above = how progress is measured
- new section = how patient activity and adherence are sustained

---

## Important Design Direction
Do **not** reuse the same 6-card grid from the original services section.

Also do **not** make this section visually identical to the FaceScan section above it.

We want visual variety while keeping the page cohesive.

This section should feel:
- a little more engaging
- still professional and clinical
- slightly more dynamic in presentation
- easy to scan

Recommended format:
- centered section heading + short intro
- a **research-backed highlight block** near the top
- below that, a **3-card layout**
- optional closing statement under the cards

This gives the section a different visual rhythm from the others while preserving consistency.

---

## Visual Style
Match the overall page aesthetic:
- clean healthcare design
- modern, trustworthy, easy to read
- rounded cards
- subtle borders/shadows
- no overly playful “gaming” look

Important:
Even though this section is about game-based activities, the design should still feel medical/professional, not gimmicky.

Suggested styling direction:
- section background: subtle contrast from surrounding section if helpful
  - ex: very light neutral or soft tinted background
- cards: white background with soft border
- border radius: `rounded-2xl`
- spacing: generous
- typography: clear hierarchy, strong readability
- optional icons if already used elsewhere on page

## Research Highlight Block
Use this as a visually distinct supporting block above the cards.

### Highlight Heading
### Backed by Cognitive Engagement Research

### Highlight Copy
Research on structured, game-like cognitive exercises has shown that consistent participation can support memory, processing, learning, and other meaningful cognitive functions over time.

This is not passive screen time — it is active therapeutic engagement designed to encourage adherence and reinforce better long-term participation.
